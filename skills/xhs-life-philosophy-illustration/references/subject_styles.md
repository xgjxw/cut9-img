# Subject styles

Use these as recurring protagonists. Keep the protagonist consistent across all nine cells, but vary pose, expression, props, and scene action.

## workplace-monk

Cute minimalist workplace monk; round white face; orange robe; simple black line limbs; calm but expressive face; soft black outline; pastel colors; clean sticker comic.

Best for: 职场低内耗, 加班边界, 稳定情绪, 长期成长.

## office-cat

Cute white office cat; tiny orange hoodie or scarf; round face; expressive eyes; simple sticker cartoon; soft black outline.

Best for: 轻松吐槽, 自我照顾, 打工人共鸣.

## round-office-worker

Simple cute office worker; round white face; orange sweater; minimal hair detail; black line limbs; clean office props.

Best for: 泛职场、效率、沟通、项目管理.

## zen-rabbit

Small white rabbit with orange vest; calm face; long ears used for expressive poses; soft pastel sticker style.

Best for: 松弛感, 情绪稳定, 人生哲学.

## tiny-robot

Small friendly robot; white body with orange accents; simple screen face; office and life props; minimal futuristic sticker style.

Best for: AI时代、效率工具、信息过载、自动化反思.

## crayon-shinchan

Custom protagonist inspired by Crayon Shin-chan: a mischievous five-year-old boy with thick eyebrows, round cheeks, simple childlike proportions, red shirt, yellow shorts, white socks, and playful expressive poses. Scene construction should match the original slice-of-life tone: home, kindergarten, playground, family room, small town street, toy box, snacks, crayons, simple everyday comedy, warm childhood innocence.

Best for: 童年回忆、小时候的梦想、反差治愈、普通人的松弛感.

## doraemon

Custom protagonist inspired by Doraemon: a round blue robotic cat with white face and belly, red nose, bell collar, small tail, and a front pocket that can hold small symbolic props. Scene construction should match the original gentle sci-fi school-life tone: tatami room, study desk, drawer, neighborhood street, school bag, simple future gadgets, friendship, childhood wish fulfillment, warm blue-and-white palette.

Best for: 童年梦想、被保护感、成长愿望、治愈幻想、友情陪伴.
